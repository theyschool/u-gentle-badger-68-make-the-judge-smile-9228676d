One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today — written to warm a judge's heart and surprise it a little.

## Entry

> My neighbour's cat, who has ignored me for two years, walked in at breakfast, sat on my foot, and stayed for the whole pot of tea.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). The output is deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical files.
